一句话说明

本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息。所有排盘运算均在你的设备本地完成，应用不与任何外部服务器通信。

一、我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。

本应用为离线运行的排盘工具。使用过程中产生的全部数据（包括你输入的日期时间、姓名备注、排盘结果与历史记录）均仅保存在你的设备本地，不会上传至开发者或任何第三方服务器。

具体而言，本应用：

  • 不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、手机号、电子邮箱等身份信息；
  • 不读取通讯录、短信、通话记录；
  • 不获取地理位置信息（真太阳时校正使用应用内置的城市经纬度表，不调用定位功能）；
  • 不访问相机、麦克风；
  • 不收集设备标识符（IMEI、Android ID、MAC 地址等）用于追踪或画像；
  • 不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、统计 SDK 或用户行为分析组件。

二、权限使用说明

应用仅申请以下权限，用途如下：

三、数据的存储与管理

  • 存储位置：所有数据（历史记录、个人设置）保存在应用私有目录内，其他应用无法访问。
  • 数据导出：你可以主动将排盘结果导出为文件。导出行为由你发起，文件保存位置由你选择，开发者无法获取这些文件。
  • 数据备份：备份文件由你自行保管，开发者不接触备份内容。
  • 数据删除：你可以随时在应用内删除单条历史记录或清空全部记录；卸载应用将一并删除应用私有目录中的全部数据，此过程不可恢复。
  • 开发者访问：由于数据不上传，开发者无法访问、查看或恢复你的任何数据。

四、数据共享与第三方

我们不与任何第三方共享你的数据。

  • 应用不集成第三方广告平台；
  • 应用不集成第三方数据分析或崩溃统计服务；
  • 应用不进行用户行为追踪；
  • 应用不向境外传输任何数据。

Privacy Policy (English Summary)

App: HaiTianZongShiPan  |  Package: net.lingbaolou.shipan
Effective date: October 13, 2026

Summary: This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information whatsoever. This is an offline divination charting tool. All data you create — input dates, notes, chart results, and history records — is stored solely on your own device and is never uploaded to the developer or any third party. The app requires no account registration, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

2. Permissions

  • INTERNET — Required by Android for the app's embedded local service, which listens only on 127.0.0.1 (localhost). The app does not communicate with any external server.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Declared by the embedded browser component; the app itself does not read network state.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Android 9 and below only; used solely to save user-initiated exports (Word documents or images).

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, calendar, or sensor permissions.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in the app's private directory, inaccessible to other apps. You may delete any record at any time within the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data permanently. Since no data is uploaded, the developer cannot access your data.

4. Third Parties

No third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs are included. No data is shared with or sold to third parties. No data is transferred internationally.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adult learners of traditional Chinese metaphysics and is not directed at children under 13. As no personal information is collected, there is no risk of children's data being compromised.

6. In-App Purchases

The app is entirely free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payment processing of any kind.

7. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date and noted in the app's release notes.

8. Disclaimer
